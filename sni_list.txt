# These are CANDIDATES. Nothing here is guaranteed to work on your network or on your Cloudflare IP:
# sni_scan.py tries every name and keeps only what really passes (stage 1) and what is really fast
# (stages 2-3). Add your own names anywhere; duplicates are ignored. Order matters only for
# --max-snis N (takes the first N), so the best-known names come first.
#
#   Part 1  "popular picks"
#   Part 2  sites that are (or often are) behind Cloudflare. They work in --mode direct AND --mode spoof.
#   Part 3  names that are NOT on Cloudflare - Iranian sites, big tech, CDNs. They only make sense as a
#           FAKE SNI with --mode spoof (sni-spoof-rs): the DPI sees the name, Cloudflare never serves it.
#           In --mode direct they are dropped in stage 1 (Cloudflare answers with an alert).
#
